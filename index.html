How we work

One team, one plan, from first sketch to final paver

Coordinating separate trades is where outdoor projects usually go wrong. Arco designs and installs in house, so the same team is accountable for the whole space.

  1. Consultation

    A designer visits your property, measures the space, and talks through how you actually want to use it — dining, entertaining, shade, pets, parking.

  2. Design & proposal

    You see the layout and the material options together — paver patterns, stone tones, coping, structures — with an itemised proposal to approve before anything starts.

  3. Preparation

    Excavation, base preparation, compaction and drainage. It is the part nobody sees and the part that decides whether the surface still looks right years from now.

  4. Installation

    Hardscaping, structures, turf and fencing are sequenced by one crew rather than handed between subcontractors, with the site cleaned down as work progresses.

  5. Walkthrough

    We walk the finished space with you, confirm every detail is as approved, and leave you with guidance on caring for the materials we installed.

Common questions

What South Florida homeowners ask us first

Which areas of South Florida do you serve?

We work across Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm Beach counties from our base in Davie. Parkland, Davie, Weston, Plantation, Fort Lauderdale, Pembroke Pines, Coral Springs and Boca Raton are among the markets we serve most often — you can see the full list on our service areas page. If your address is nearby but not listed, call and ask.

Is Arco Outdoors licensed and insured?

Yes. We hold Florida Certified Building Contractor license CBC1269393 and carry insurance. The license number appears in our footer on every page so you can verify it independently before you hire anyone — something worth doing with any contractor you consider.

Can one contractor really handle an entire outdoor remodel?

That is the reason Arco exists. Rather than hiring a paver installer, a carpenter for the pergola, a separate turf company and a fencing crew — then coordinating them yourself — one team designs and builds the whole space. We cover paver installation, patios, driveways, pool decks, outdoor kitchens, pergolas, tiki huts, artificial turf, fencing and impact windows & doors.

What happens during the free consultation?

A designer comes to your property, measures the area, and walks you through the material options that suit it. You leave with an understanding of what the space could become and a written estimate — with no obligation to proceed.

What about permits?

Permitting requirements differ from one South Florida municipality to the next, and they depend on what you are building — a paver patio and a roofed structure are not treated the same way. We review what applies to your specific project during the on-site consultation, so there are no surprises after you have committed.
