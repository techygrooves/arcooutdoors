What you will get

What happens when you get in touch

  1. A person, not a queue

    Calls during published hours reach us directly. There is no call centre between you and the people who would do the work.

  2. A straight reply

    Occasionally that means telling you the job is not ours to do, or that half of what you had in mind would serve you better than all of it.

  3. A visit before any number

    We do not price outdoor work from photographs. Levels, drainage, access and what is under the existing surface all have to be seen.

  4. Something in writing

    A document you can hold us to, and compare against other quotes — it names the exclusions as carefully as the inclusions, and says who carries out each part.

What we will not do

No high-pressure close, no discount that expires at the end of the visit, no signature asked for on the day. If a contractor needs your decision before they leave your driveway, that is information about the contractor.

Common questions

Before you get in touch

What is the fastest way to reach you?

The phone, during published hours — 305-951-8862, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. A call gets a real conversation about whether we are the right contractor for your project, which is faster than any exchange of messages. Email reaches the same place at jonah@arcooutdoors.com and suits questions with detail or photographs attached. Outside those hours, leave a message or send an email and it will be picked up the next working day.

Is the address a showroom I can visit?

It is our published business address in Davie, not a retail showroom, so please call before coming rather than arriving expecting a display area. For choosing materials, a supplier's yard is a far better place to see product at scale than any showroom would be, and we would rather meet you at your property, where the levels, access and existing conditions that actually govern a project can be seen.

Do you charge for the first visit?

No. The consultation and the written proposal that follows it are free and carry no obligation. What we will not do is quote a project without seeing it, because the things that decide an outdoor job — how the ground falls, where water goes, what is under the existing surface, how machinery reaches the rear — cannot be established from photographs or a satellite image.

Which areas do you cover?

The three counties above the fold on every page of this site — Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm Beach — reached from the Davie office. Eight markets have their own pages because we build in them often enough to write something useful: Parkland, Davie, Weston, Plantation, Fort Lauderdale, Pembroke Pines, Coral Springs and Boca Raton. If your address sits in those counties and is not on that list, that is a gap in our writing rather than in our coverage — call and ask.

How do I check that you are licensed?

Our Florida Certified Building Contractor licence number is CBC1269393, and it appears in the footer of every page on this site so it can be checked against the state record independently. Verifying a licence number is worth doing with any contractor you are considering, and a contractor who is reluctant to give you theirs has told you something useful.

Can you tell me what permits my project needs?

Not over the phone, and treat anyone who tries as a warning sign. What is required depends on the jurisdiction and on how large the job is, and two neighbouring cities can answer the question differently. The reliable answer comes from the reviewing authority itself, which we approach on your behalf once we know the address and the scope — and we take the paperwork on for whatever we build. An association, if one governs your property, is a second and separate review with its own pace.
