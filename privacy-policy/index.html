Privacy Policy

Written from an audit of what this website actually does — not from a template. Last updated 2 September 2026.

The short version

This website sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, runs no analytics and carries no advertising or tracking scripts. There is no login, no account, no payment and no mailing list.

Its three forms currently send nothing to any server. When you submit one, your browser opens a pre-filled message in your own email application — we receive it only if you press send there, and it arrives as an ordinary email.

Two pages load photographs from a third-party image service; that is the one automatic request to anyone other than our own site, and it is described below. Everything else on the page — fonts, styles, scripts, images — is served from this domain.

The only place you give us anything

The forms, and where what you type actually goes

There are three forms: the project enquiry on Get a Quote, the question form on Contact, and a short consultation form near the foot of the homepage.

What each one asks for

  • Project enquiry: first name, last name, phone, email, the ZIP code the work would happen in, the type of project, and a description. Optionally a preferred contact method and a rough timescale.
  • Contact question: name, email, an optional phone number, and your question.
  • Homepage consultation: name, phone, email, a service of interest and any project details.

None of them asks for a street address, a budget, a date of birth, an account number or any financial detail, and none should be used to send documents. There is nothing at enquiry stage that needs them.

What happens when you press submit — today

Each form has an empty submission endpoint, which means the page transmits nothing to us or to anyone else. Instead your browser is handed a pre-addressed draft message and your own email application opens with the details filled in. Nothing reaches us until you send that message yourself, and when you do, it travels through your email provider and ours exactly like any other email you write. The success panel on the page says this rather than pretending the message has already been delivered.

A practical consequence worth knowing: because the enquiry becomes an ordinary email, your email address and whatever you wrote sit in your own sent folder and in our inbox, subject to your provider's terms and ours.

If that changes

We may connect the forms to a form-handling service so that submissions post directly rather than routing through your mail application. That is a single configuration change, and if it is made this page will be updated to name the provider before the change goes live. As of the date at the top of this page, no such service is connected and none is receiving data.

What we do with an enquiry

We read it, reply to it, and keep it as ordinary business correspondence for as long as it is useful — a record of what was discussed, which matters if a project follows. It is not sold, rented, traded or passed to a marketing company. Nobody gets subscribed to anything either — no such list exists to be added to.

The exception

Images loaded from another company

The homepage and the services page each display six photographs that are loaded from Unsplash's image service rather than from this domain. Every other image on the site is served from here.

What that means in practice. When one of those two pages loads, your browser makes a request to that company's servers to fetch the pictures. Any such request necessarily reveals your IP address, the page you are on and your browser's user-agent to the company serving the file — that is how the web works, and it is true of every externally hosted image anywhere. We do not receive that information, we cannot see it, and we have no arrangement with them about it.

What we are doing about it. Those twelve images are the last remnant of the site's first build. Every other photograph has been converted and moved onto this domain, and the intention is to do the same with these so that a visit to this site involves no third-party request at all. Until that is finished, it would be misleading to tell you the site makes no external requests, so this section exists.

If you would rather avoid the request in the meantime, a content blocker will stop it and the two pages will still work — you will simply see empty image areas where those six photographs sit.

Outbound links

The contact page carries a link that opens our address in Google Maps. It is a plain link: nothing loads from Google unless you click it, and once you do you are on their site under their privacy policy rather than ours.

The same applies to any other external link on this site. We do not use link-tracking redirects.

Beyond the page

Hosting and server records

This site is a set of static files — HTML, CSS, images and one script — served by a hosting provider. Like effectively every web server in existence, a host may record technical information about the requests it serves: the IP address making the request, the time, the file requested, the response status and the browser's user-agent string. That kind of logging is a function of how web servers operate and is used for delivery, diagnostics and abuse prevention.

We do not use those records for marketing, we do not build profiles from them, and we do not combine them with anything you send us. We have no analytics layer sitting on top of them, which is why we cannot tell you how many people read any particular page.

Your information

Asking us what we have, and asking us to delete it

Different laws give people different rights over personal information, and which of them apply to you depends on where you live and on facts about our business that this page is not the right place to characterise. So rather than list statutes we would be claiming to satisfy, here is the practical position:

Write to us and we will act on it. If you have sent us an enquiry and you want to know what we hold, want a copy of it, want it corrected, or want it deleted, email jonah@arcooutdoors.com or call 305-951-8862 and say so. We will do it, and we will tell you when it is done. You do not need to cite a law and you do not need a reason.

The one limit is ordinary and worth stating: where an enquiry has become a project, some records may need to be kept as part of the business record of that work.

Children

This site is aimed at homeowners considering building work. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us something, tell us and it will be deleted.

Security

Enquiries reach us as ordinary email, which is not an encrypted channel end to end. That is worth knowing, and it is another reason the forms deliberately do not ask for anything sensitive. We take reasonable care with what we hold, and — as anyone honest will tell you — no method of transmitting or storing information is completely secure.

Changes to this notice

If the site's behaviour changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top moves. The two changes most likely to require that are connecting the forms to a submission service and removing the last third-party images. Both are described above so you can see them coming.
